# Cameroonian Okok Recipe (Text Version)

Okok is a traditional Cameroonian dish made with shredded okok leaves, palm oil, and other ingredients. It can be prepared in different ways depending on the region and family tradition.

## Ingredients

- 500 g shredded okok leaves
- 200–300 g groundnut paste or ground roasted peanuts
- 200–300 ml red palm oil
- 200 g smoked fish
- 200 g meat, optional
- 100 g crayfish, ground
- 2–3 seasoning cubes
- Pepper to taste
- Salt to taste
- Water, as needed
- Sugar, optional, depending on your preferred taste

## Preparation

1\. Prepare the leaves: Wash the shredded okok leaves thoroughly and drain them.

2\. Cook the leaves: Put the leaves in a pot with a little water and cook until they soften. Stir occasionally and add small amounts of water if necessary.

3\. Prepare the groundnut mixture: If using groundnut paste, mix it with a little warm water until smooth. If using ground roasted peanuts, grind them into a fine paste.

4\. Add the groundnuts: Stir the groundnut mixture into the softened okok leaves. Mix thoroughly to distribute it evenly.

5\. Add the fish and meat: Add the smoked fish, cooked meat if desired, and ground crayfish. Stir well.

6\. Add palm oil: Pour in the red palm oil and mix thoroughly. Allow the mixture to cook over low to medium heat.

7\. Season: Add seasoning cubes, pepper, and salt to taste. Some households add a little sugar to balance the flavour.

8\. Simmer: Cook for another 15–25 minutes, stirring regularly to prevent sticking. If the mixture becomes too thick, add a small amount of water.

9\. Finish cooking: The okok is ready when the leaves are tender, the groundnut mixture is well cooked, and the palm oil is evenly incorporated.

## How to serve

Okok is commonly served with:

- Cassava sticks (miondo)
- Boiled cassava
- Bobolo
- Water fufu, depending on local tradition

Tip: The exact recipe varies between Cameroonian communities. Some versions use groundnuts and palm oil, while others include sugar or other ingredients to achieve their characteristic sweet and savoury flavour.
