## Ingredients

- 500 g shredded eru leaves (okok)
- 1 kg waterleaf
- 500 g beef, cow skin (canda), or assorted meat
- Smoked fish, dried fish, or stockfish
- 250–500 ml red palm oil, depending on your preference
- 2–4 seasoning cubes
- Salt to taste
- Fresh pepper or dry pepper to taste
- Crayfish, ground (optional)
- Water, as needed

## Instructions

1\. Prepare the meat: Wash the meat and cook with salt, seasoning cubes, and a little water until tender. Cook the stockfish separately if it needs more time to soften.

2\. Prepare the leaves: Wash the waterleaf thoroughly and chop it. Rinse the shredded eru according to the package instructions, then drain it.

3\. Cook the waterleaf: Put the waterleaf in a large pot and cook until it releases its liquid and softens.

4\. Add the eru: Add the shredded eru to the waterleaf and stir well. Allow it to cook for about 10–15 minutes, stirring occasionally.

5\. Add the meat and fish: Add the cooked meat, smoked fish, stockfish, and crayfish if using. Mix thoroughly.

6\. Add palm oil: Pour in the red palm oil and stir until the leaves are evenly coated.

7\. Season the dish: Add pepper, seasoning cubes, and salt to taste. Add a little meat stock if necessary, but avoid making the eru too watery.

8\. Simmer: Cook for another 10–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the leaves are tender and the oil is well incorporated.

## How to serve

Serve hot with:

- Yellow garri (eba)
- Water fufu
- Corn fufu

Tip: For authentic Cameroonian-style eru, use plenty of palm oil, avoid adding too much water, and let the leaves cook thoroughly. The finished dish should be rich, well-seasoned, and moist rather than watery.
