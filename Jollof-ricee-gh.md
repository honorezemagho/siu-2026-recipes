# Ghanaian Jollof Rice Recipe

## 1. Introduction

Ghanaian Jollof Rice is a popular West African dish made with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions, and spices. It is known for its rich tomato flavour, vibrant red-orange colour, and aromatic seasoning. It is commonly served with chicken, beef, fish, or fried plantains.

## 2. Preparation Time

- Preparation time: 20 minutes
- Cooking time: 45–60 minutes
- Total time: Approximately 1 hour 20 minutes
- Servings: 4–6 people

## 3. Ingredients

### Main Ingredients
- 3 cups long-grain rice
- 5 medium tomatoes
- 2 red bell peppers
- 2 medium onions
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 1/2 cup vegetable oil
- 3 cups chicken stock or water, plus more if needed
- 2 cloves garlic
- 1 small piece of ginger
- 2 seasoning cubes, optional
- 1 teaspoon curry powder
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 bay leaf, optional
- Salt to taste
- Fresh hot pepper, to taste

### Optional Accompaniments
- Fried or grilled chicken
- Fried ripe plantains
- Salad
- Grilled fish

## 4. Preparation Instructions

### Step 1: Prepare the Rice
1. Rinse the rice thoroughly under clean water.
2. Drain and set aside.

### Step 2: Prepare the Tomato Blend
1. Wash the tomatoes, bell peppers, and one onion.
2. Peel the garlic and ginger.
3. Blend the tomatoes, bell peppers, garlic, ginger, and hot pepper until smooth.
4. Set aside.

### Step 3: Prepare the Stew Base
1. Heat the vegetable oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Chop the remaining onion and add it to the oil.
3. Fry until softened.
4. Add the tomato paste and cook for approximately 5 minutes, stirring regularly.
5. Pour in the blended tomato mixture.
6. Cook uncovered for 15–20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the mixture thickens and excess liquid reduces.

### Step 4: Add the Seasonings
1. Add the curry powder, thyme, bay leaf, seasoning cubes, and salt.
2. Stir thoroughly.
3. Add the chicken stock or water.
4. Bring the mixture to a gentle boil.

### Step 5: Add the Rice
1. Add the washed rice to the tomato sauce.
2. Stir to distribute the sauce evenly.
3. Cover the pot with a tight-fitting lid.
4. Reduce the heat to low and cook for approximately 25–35 minutes.
5. Check occasionally and add a little hot water if the rice is still firm and the pot is dry.
6. Avoid excessive stirring to prevent the rice from becoming mushy.

### Step 6: Finish Cooking
1. When the rice is tender and the liquid has been absorbed, remove the pot from the heat.
2. Allow the rice to rest, covered, for 5–10 minutes.
3. Fluff gently with a fork.
4. Remove the bay leaf before serving.

## 5. Serving Suggestions

Serve Ghanaian Jollof Rice with:
- Grilled or fried chicken
- Fried ripe plantains
- Grilled fish
- Fresh vegetable salad

## 6. Tips for Delicious Jollof Rice

- Cook the tomato mixture thoroughly to remove its raw taste.
- Use stock for a richer flavour.
- Cook the rice over low heat to prevent burning.
- Add liquid gradually to avoid soggy rice.
- Adjust the pepper according to your preference.

## 7. Storage

Refrigerate leftovers in a covered container within two hours of cooking. Consume within 3–4 days and reheat thoroughly before serving.

## 8. Conclusion

Ghanaian Jollof Rice is a flavourful tomato-based rice dish that combines aromatic spices with a rich stew. It is suitable for family meals, celebrations, and special occasions.
